HW4: Hierarchical Robot Arm

A chained hierarchy: Base → Shoulder/Lower Arm → Elbow/Upper Arm → Wrist → Gripper Fingers (6 levels).
Drag each slider below to pose the arm - every joint carries all of its children with it.
Use mouse to drag and rotate the view. Use arrow keys and w, s to move the camera.
Press F12 to see the console for debugging.
